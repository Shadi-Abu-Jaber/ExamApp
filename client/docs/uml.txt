==============================================================================
KEY RELATIONSHIPS
==============================================================================
ServiceRegistry  ─▶ creates ─▶ Config, Logger, Storage, Notify
                 ─▶ creates ─▶ MockDb(storage, logger)
                 ─▶ creates ─▶ ExamService(db, config, logger)
                 ─▶ creates ─▶ SubmissionService(db, config, logger)
                 ─▶ creates ─▶ AuthService(db, storage, notify, logger)

ExamService.create()          ─▶ inserts Exam into MockDb('exams')
ExamService.setStatus()       ─▶ updates Exam.status in MockDb
SubmissionService.submit()    ─▶ loads Exam, computes score, inserts Submission
AuthService.login/register()  ─▶ reads/writes 'users' in MockDb;
                                  writes 'current_user' via Storage
