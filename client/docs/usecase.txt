PRE/POST CONDITIONS (selected)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UC-6 Create a new exam
  Pre:  Teacher is signed in.
  Post: Exam exists in 'exams' table with status="draft" and createdBy=teacher.id.

UC-8 Publish a draft exam
  Pre:  Exam.isPublishable() (title not empty and every question isValid()).
  Post: Exam.status="published" — appears for students in UC-12.

UC-13 Take an exam
  Pre:  Student is signed in; exam status="published".
  Post: A Submission row is inserted; score/total are persisted; student is
        redirected to a pass/fail card and the entry appears in UC-14.
